# Skill sound effects: prompts and wiring

Updated: 2026-10-18 · Written: 2026-10-18 · [한국어](Skill_Sfx_Prompts_20261018.md)

All 30 active skills of the D4 skill tree already had a base sound effect, but all 84 modifiers sounded exactly like their skill. Every modifier now has a sound of its own. Of the 84 modifiers, 22 inherit an existing sound from a retired skill (they play in combat for the first time), and **62 were created with ElevenLabs on 2026-10-18 and inserted**. The same day, 5 of the inherited sounds (`W02-1`, `W06-3`, `A03-3`, `M02-3`, `M07-3`) and 3 base sounds (`A06`, `A18`, `M07`, including the follow-up hits `A03-3.hit`, `A18.hit`, `M02-3.hit`) were **regenerated and their masters replaced** (14 clips). This page lists, per skill, which sound plays when and which prompt made the new ones.

## How they were made

- Model `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`, prompt influence 0.65, no looping, a duration per sound (0.8–3.2 s requested). One take per modifier. Only three clips whose first take was nearly silent or ended without a decay (`A18-1`, `M08-1`, `M11-2`) were re-prompted once.
- Prompts follow the house style of the existing effects: one sound source, its material and texture, and the closing phrase "isolated with a clear attack and controlled natural decay." One generation holds one sound; no visual vocabulary.
- The original MP3 files are kept byte for byte in `AudioSources/ElevenLabs/Sources/`; `tools/elevenlabs_audio.py` makes the masters (48 kHz mono PCM, a per-cue LUFS target, true peak -2 dBTP). Prompts, generation ids and hashes are in the [provenance manifest](../../AudioSources/ElevenLabs/manifest.json); the plan and the outcome are in the [plan file](../../AudioSources/ElevenLabs/skill-sfx-d4-plan.json).
- ElevenLabs flow `tcHr1hKn6p0at4pzyUOg`, 354.6 credits (including the three re-takes), 89.7 s of new audio.
- The 14 replacement clips were made in flow `81oyAOAv3bzfGC0fFupM`, one take per runtime variation with the plan's prompts unchanged, 64.0 credits (including one re-take), 16.8 s in total. Clip file names were kept and the same files overwritten (for example `skill_W11_1.wav` is the bank cue `skill.W02-1`). The first take of `skill.M02-3.hit` mastered 10 LU under its target (the old master sat at -20.2 LUFS, the first take at -25.2), so it was re-prompted once with a low thump and a shard cascade added. The rejected first take was neither adopted nor kept.

## How they play

- The cue id is `skill.<skill>-<modifier number>`. When the cast's modifier has its own cue, it plays **at the moment the moment table gives** (`GameAudio.ModifierCue`, `GameAudio.Moments`); otherwise the skill's cue plays. The moment of all 84 modifiers is in the "moment" column below, and `SkillModifierAudioTests` pins all 84 in the code and in this document (Korean and English).
- The rule is **the moment of the event the sound holds**. The real moments were read from the `ClassSkillEvent` and `effectEvents` the simulation records and from the effect code in `Runtime/Core/SkillTreeD4`. There are three kinds.
  - **Cast** (the default, 69): the modifier changes the sound of the cast itself. Its cue replaces the skill's cue in the same moment (cast, take-off, whirlwind start).
  - **Slot** (6: `start`, `land`, `hit`): the modifier cue replaces the skill's cue of that slot. `start` is the wind-up start (`W08-1`, `W18-1`, `M17-2`), `land` the landing (`W02-2`), `hit` the follow-up hit (`W17-2`, `A18-2`, with the same 0.25 s gap as the skill's own follow-up cue).
  - **Event** (9, `kind:source`): the skill's own cue still sounds at the cast, and the modifier cue is added when the simulation reports the event. Kinds are `DAMAGE` (source `EXTRA:W03` is secondary damage, `A05` the skill's own damage), `CASHOUT`, `EFFECT` and `STATUS`. `*` plays for every event (at most once per 0.3 s), otherwise once per cast; `+` skips the cast's own first event (the mark itself). An event sounds when the damage or status really happens: an explosion with no enemy to hit makes no sound.
- Follow-up damage (trap bursts, the crossbow turret, the returning frost orb) plays a modifier sound only when the modifier has a `.hit` cue; each cue's cooldown keeps overlaps in check.
- The moments were set from simulation events and prompts, without listening. To change one after listening, edit the line in `GameAudio.Moments`, the table below and the table in `SkillModifierAudioTests` together.

## Checks

- All 62 new clips and the 14 replacement clips have a complete provenance record (original, hash, prompt) and pass the signal rules (no clipping, silent edges, DC, true peak). Whether all 84 modifiers have a cue, the moment table of all 84 (including that the Korean and English tables here match the code), the cue choice per moment, and playback and repeat gaps from simulation events are covered by the Unity Edit Mode tests (`SkillModifierAudioTests`).
- Nobody has listened to them. These ten are mostly short impacts, so peak limiting leaves them 4 LU or more under their loudness target: `skill.W04-3`, `skill.M08-1`, `skill.A02-3`, `skill.A06-3`, `skill.A05-3`, `skill.M06-1`, `skill.M06-3`, `skill.M17-2`, `skill.W18-1`, `skill.M02-3.hit` (target -15, actual -21.5 LUFS; -20.2 before the replacement). The other replacement clips are within 1.2 LU of their target (only `skill.A03-3` is 3.3 LU under, as before) and pass the clipping, edge, DC and true-peak rules.
- The existing failures of `generate_sfx.py --check` and `elevenlabs_audio.py --check` (the catalog and the bank disagree after the D4 skill-id migration) predate this change and remain. The 62 new catalog and bank rows use the generator's format.

## Deferred

- The 14 old original MP3s (`AudioSources/ElevenLabs/Sources/`) that left the manifest with the replacement were deleted the same day after the user confirmed (recoverable from git history). The 27 unreferenced original MP3s that were already there independent of this replacement were left alone.
- `W06-2` (Frenzy Cry) never cast in its example fight, so no presentation recording exists for it. Its sound and effects are wired.
