# Town errands (the armor errand), forge cleanup and tutorial fixes (2026-10-21)

Record of the 16 requested items. Korean original: [Town_Errands_Armor_Forge_20261021.md](Town_Errands_Armor_Forge_20261021.md).

## 1. Skill window and area attacks (items 1–3)

- **Class buttons removed:** the game's skill window (`SkillTreeD4Window`) is opened with the store and no longer draws the class button row at the top (`d4-tab-0/1/2`; `showClassTabs=store==null`). Design-preview and diagnostic hosts (no store) still draw it. Each hero sees only its own class's skill window. `SkillTreeD4StoreHostTests` checks the buttons are gone.
- **Raking Wound and the spin:** the cause was a target cap in W09. A `Take(5)` bled only the first five enemies in the area by id, so with six or more some never bled. The cap is gone: every enemy inside the cone (the spin's 360° circle) and radius bleeds. The single-target variant (Deep Wound) still hits one enemy. `RakingWoundHitsEveryEnemyInItsAreaAndNoOtherEnemy` (normal and spin) checks all seven enemies in the area bleed and the two behind or outside do not.

## 2. Tutorial wording and positions (items 4–5)

- **Potions:** two lines now say potion use is automatic and that when HP drops below the level set in potion settings and potions remain, one is drunk. Because the current setting only drinks at 1% or lower, the next line asks the player to set 30% or higher in the lower-left potion settings and save (the 30–100% save range is unchanged).
- **First-engagement and in-fight position:** the ranged pack (`GuideTutorial.RangedPack`) no longer scatters around the pillars; it stands together (within about 2 m). The hero going to the pack's centre is not hard-coded: it is the real `Pack center` edict (`position.mode=CENTER`, `EdictPackCenterGoal`) at work, and `GuideHazardYardTests` checks the hero comes within 2.5 m of the pack centre and never backs off more than 6 m.

## 3. Display and gear cards (items 6, 8–11)

- **Level-up font (6):** the title and level number use the casual display font (`UiSkin.Casual.LabelFont`), the subtitle and confirm text the casual body font (`LevelUpCelebration`).
- **Wait after the treasure goblin (8):** the loot lesson starts 2 seconds after the goblin dies (`GuideTutorial.LootLessonSeconds=2`).
- **Gear recommendation card size (9):** the mockup card (92 px wide, 0.75× in landscape) now grows with the screen. Card, text and icons are built at the scale `k` from the start so text stays sharp (no `localScale`). It is 1× at 440 px portrait width and larger on wider screens.
- **Gear card lesson order (10):** item picked up → recommendation card appears → a highlight ring blinks on the card for 2 seconds → Velina speaks (`GuideGearBeatWaits`, `GuideGearFlashSeconds=2`). The line waits for the blink to end. The helmet, rare sword and lower sword lessons all do this.
- **Quest arrow (11):** the three cubes that read as a yellow "Y" are replaced by one flat arrow (shaft + triangular head) with a navy outline copy underneath, so it reads on sand and grass (`WorldView.TownGuide.cs`).

## 4. Quest flow (items 12, 15, 16)

Saved value `AccountGuide.townQuest`: 0 none, 1 talk to Anton, 2 enhance the weapon, 3 done, **4 get armor, 5 rift level 1**. 1–3 are already-saved values and are unchanged; the order is `TownQuest.Next` (0→1→2→4→5→3). New save fields are `townEnhanced` (the weapon was enhanced) and `townArmorGiven` (the gift armor was taken); a save without them reads false.

| Step | Goal line | Guide | Rift |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 Enhance weapon | Enhance your weapon at the forge | blacksmith Marc | locked (Anton: "go to the forge first") |
| 4 Armor | Buy armor and put it on | weapon merchant Jake Bokun | locked (Anton: "a better weapon alone won't do…") |
| 5 Rift level 1 | Clear rift level 1 | rift keeper Anton | open |

- **Forge enhancement lesson (12):** when the enhance tab opens, everything is dimmed except the `+1 level` button. It uses the same `PrologueGate` as the gear lessons (canvas sort 2000), so it draws above the window, and if the button stays missing for over two seconds the dim lets go so the player cannot get stuck (`TownStep` in `GameUI.TownErrands.cs`). The old ring is gone.
- **Blacksmith's send-off (15):** a successful enhancement only saves `townEnhanced`; the quest stays at 2. When the forge window closes Marc says it is not enough even for rift level 1, to go to the weapon shop, that he has put in a word so one piece of armor should be free, and to wear one more before the rift. The choice `Head to the weapon shop` moves the quest to 4. Closing early repeats it next time (as the chief's welcome does).
- **Jake's gift (16):** at 4 the first talk with the weapon merchant plays five lines (so you're the one Marc mentioned → going into the rift in that gear? → heroes who last long keep the shop fed → my treat → it's at the top of the display), then `Look at the display` opens the equipment shop's Buy tab. The lines were rewritten in Jake's voice (stir-fry metaphors, hearty speech).
- **0-gold gift armor:** when 4 opens, a fixed magic iron armor per hero (`GuideTutorial.ArmorKind`, base B13, affixes AF03 and AF01) is placed first in the shop list at price 0 (`ShopOffer.gift`). A restock keeps it first until it is taken and never offers it again afterwards (`EquipmentShop.EnsureGift`, `townArmorGiven`). Taking it is not a purchase: it spends no gold, ignores the shop's purchase budget (`WithinBudget`) and leaves no purchase record, and the hero puts it on himself. Its id uses `/town/`, so restarting the guide stage does not take it back.
- **Buy and wear steps:** with the shop open, the screen is dimmed to one control at a time: `Buy` tab → the gift row → `Buy` → close the shop → the Character button → the armor in the bag → `Equip`. Each step reads what is saved and on screen (no counter). Once the armor is worn, or is gone from the bag (sold), the step counts as done (`TownQuest.ArmorSettled`).
- **Jake's send-off:** with the windows closed, two lines (rift level 1 looks doable now, come back alive and shop again) end with `Head for the rift`, which sets 5. The goal line and the guide arrow now point at Anton.
- **Entering the rift and finishing:** at 5 neither the rift keeper nor entry is blocked. Entering the first rift (`BeginRun`, resuming excluded) sets the quest to 3 (done).

## 5. Forge (items 13, 14)

- **Equipment craft tab (13) — removed (user decision, 2026-10-21):** the fifth tab is no longer used, so the forge is back to four tabs (option change, slot enhancement, equipment enhancement, core craft). The user then said the whole "rare equipment crafting" feature is unwanted, so besides the tab screen (`BlacksmithWindow.CraftScreen.cs`) and its transaction `GameStore.CraftRareEquipment`, the shop page's `Rare craft` button, `ContentUnlocks.RareCraft` (UL04, including its `ContentUnlocks.json` definition), the "Rare equipment crafting" tutorial and practice, `ContentServices.Purchase` mode 1 (kept as a refused number), and the related texts, tests and documents were deleted. Gear crafted earlier stays as ordinary items, and `UL04_RARE_CRAFT` values in old saves are read and ignored (no save structure or key changed). The shop's "Craft equipment" page was left with only a gambler route, a core-craft route and a codex button, so it was useless and the town-menu button and the page itself (`GameUI.ShowShop`, `RenderItemShop`) were removed too. The gambler is the town NPC (`TownStation.Gambler`), core crafting is reached through the blacksmith NPC and the bag's enhance/reroll buttons, and the legendary/set codex (`ShowItemCollection`) still opens from the content-unlock screen and the bag.
- **Tab locks (14):** the guide stage opens all content at graduation, so no lock was ever visible. A graduated account's (`everythingOpen`) forge now follows each service's rift stage again (`ContentUnlocks.ForgeHas`): Equipment Enhancement 1, Slot Enhancement 5, Option Change 35, Core Craft 50 (values from `ContentUnlocks.json`). The only exception is the first enhancement of town errand step 2. A locked tab shows its padlock and tapping it explains the condition in a bubble (the existing `ShowServiceHint`). If every service is locked the existing sealed-forge screen shows. The engine (`Blacksmith.cs`, `Economy.cs`, `CoreCrafting.cs`, `ContentServices`, `GameStore.ForgeAccess`) applies the same rule, so it cannot be bypassed outside the screen. Older accounts that never graduated keep the access they were given.
