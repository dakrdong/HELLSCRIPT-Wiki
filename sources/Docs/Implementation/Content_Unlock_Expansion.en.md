# Content unlock implementation and validation

2026-09-12. HELLSCRIPT v0.1 test draft; these are not externally verified game values.

The actual project code, existing GDD and original workbook were updated. The current GDD was extended, not replaced with its attachment snapshot. The workbook preserves all 25 original sheets, 23 tables, 3,397 existing populated cells, formulas, styles and controls, and adds two sheets for unlock rules and U01–U30 validation. Runtime and workbook rules originate in `Assets/HELLSCRIPT/Resources/ContentUnlocks.json`.

Account services use the maximum actual rewarded clear across heroes (A). Training opens after the first ended attempt, including failure. Enhancement/offline open at A≥1, rare crafting at 3, gems at 5 or first legitimate acquisition, rerolls at 8, unidentified purchases at 10, sweep at 12, and core crafting at 15 or ten cores from one slot. Access is permanent and separate from guide completion. Sweep execution requires the selected hero's own H≥1 and uses H for rewards. New passive slots open at hero levels 3/6/10; legacy three-slot access and legal loadouts remain. Active skill levels and the four-skill limit are unchanged.

Run stage R controls encounters: basic first-theme melee enemies and BOSS01 at 1–5; existing mixed roles at 6; second theme and BOSS02 join at 11; up to two compatible elite traits at 20; BOSS03 joins at 21. Existing layout validation, encounter budgets, prior candidates and compatible trait rules remain. A single eligible boss can repeat; previous-boss exclusion remains when multiple candidates exist. Existing suspended runs are never regenerated.

Saved version-zero unlock state migrates once, retaining services universally available in the previous version, purchased capacity and three passive slots. Newly activated offline rewards never include pre-unlock time. Same-slot cores are not consumed to unlock a menu, and consuming them during crafting never revokes access. Legendary dismantling remains available before crafting unlocks. UI and execution paths both check permissions. Portal equipment/enhancement restrictions remain. Guides are optional, replayable and stored separately; first legendary/set guidance is retained independently of equipment permission.

The existing rarity source files and probability worksheet are preserved. Reroll cost remains `500*L+50*L*(L-1)` for `L=max(1,itemLevel)`, regardless of attempt count. At L=8, attempts 1/10/100 each cost 6,800 gold. Existing shop probabilities, prices and material costs remain. Existing in-progress dungeon, edict, gate, objective and concurrent localization changes were retained. Project settings match the initial working-copy snapshots. No packages, scenes or prefabs were changed, and no remote publishing or push was performed.

Final Unity Edit Mode suite: **1362 passed, zero failed**, including 37 unlock tests. A separate focused run passed 165 tests. Final macOS development build succeeded with zero errors. Automated native UI checks passed in Korean/English, portrait/landscape, covering locked services, failure-based training access, hero switching, personal sweep restrictions, guide skip/replay and gem dependency messaging. Seven screenshots were saved and reviewed. The final UI layout adjustment was subsequently compiled and exercised in the final build. Workbook formula and preservation checks passed using Artifact; native Microsoft Excel recalculation was not run.

Three fresh level-one heroes ran the actual stage-one simulation at seed 112358. Warrior failed at 80.45 seconds (45 kills, level 3), Ranger at 300 seconds (80 kills, level 5), and Mage at 42.15 seconds (24 kills, level 3). Navigation reported no errors. These results do not establish early-game balance or unavoidable-pattern fairness. Manual playtesting and Android/iOS device testing remain outstanding.

First enhancement requires 200 gold and 20 materials; stage-one boss plus first-clear rewards supply 1,950 gold and 15 materials, leaving five materials to acquire through existing chests/dismantling. Stage-three rare crafting needs 1,500 gold and 50 materials. Rerolls require suitable affixes and gold; stage-fifteen core access does not guarantee ten same-slot cores. No free currencies, gems, recipes or stat buffs were invented.

**Remaining dependencies:** real gem items, acquisition/consumption, sockets and synthesis recipes/results are absent from the existing project. Access state, a future acquisition hook and an explicit unavailable menu are implemented; gem execution is not. Authenticated account/cloud infrastructure is also absent. `MergeVerified` is a union boundary for already-verified entitlements, not a live server or cross-device solution. Actual multi-device/offline conflict testing remains unperformed.

See [the Korean report](Content_Unlock_Expansion.md) for the full condition table, U01–U30 outcomes and absolute changed-file links. Evidence is under `Artifacts/Validation/ContentUnlock/`; the runnable development build is `Builds/ContentUnlock/HELLSCRIPT.app`.
