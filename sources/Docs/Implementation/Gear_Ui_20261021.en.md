## U4 · Forge gear enhancement sparks (report 2)

**Request.** A successful enhancement makes sparks fly on the numbers it changes. The user's answer: every value that changes with enhancement gets the effect, starting with the gear enhancement screen. Extending it to other forge services is left to the user.

**Implementation.**
- `ForgeStatSpark` (`BlacksmithWindow.Gear.cs`) scatters nine gold four-point stars around one number (or the right end of the gauge) for 0.7 s and then destroys itself. It follows `Time.unscaledTime`, redraws only while alive, builds its vertices by one fixed rule and allocates nothing per frame.
- `CommitGear` records `sparkItem/sparkFrom/sparkTo/sparkStart` only right after `store.EnhanceEquipment` succeeds. A failure, reopening, an ordinary save or a language switch never passes that line.
- On every redraw (`DrawGearScreen` → `GearCard`, `DrawGearDetail`, `GearPortrait`, `DrawGearDock`) `SparkAge(item)` attaches sparks only while the record is 0.0–1.2 s old and matches the item and its enhancement level. A redraw inside the window resumes the same phase instead of replaying; once it expires, or the selection or tab changes, the record is cleared.
- Numbers covered: the `Enhancement +N` line, the gauge end, current ability, next enhancement (skipped at +100 because the number stays the same), accumulated enhancement bonus, and outside portrait the `+N` badge, the list card's stat and `+N`, and the dock's level line. The pre-enhancement value, the per-level gain, the growth preview and the quote buttons do not change or are not stat numbers, so they are left out.
- With reduce-motion (`StoryDialogueWindow.ReduceMotion`) the same numbers get a still gold underline for 1.2 s. Nothing moves or blinks. Every spark has `raycastTarget=false`, so reading and clicking are unaffected.

**Verification.** `ForgeGearSparkTests` (new), `ForgePresentationAllocationTests` and `BlacksmithCasualSkinTests` ran in Edit Mode. The new tests check that the vertices stay near the number and turn transparent at the end, that reduce-motion is still, that nothing allocates after warm-up, and success, save failure, max level, consecutive enhancements, selection change/reopen, expiry and reduce-motion. The existing ring baseline checks do not stand in for the new effect.

- Run: 27 of 27 Edit Mode tests passed (`ForgeGearSparkTests` 11, `ForgePresentationAllocationTests` 7, `BlacksmithCasualSkinTests` 9), batch mode, no compile errors. `check_ui_contract.py --verify` passed 15 tests and `check_ui_refresh.py` passed 14 bindings. Full Edit Mode was not run.
- Captures: `GearUiEvidence20261021/` holds `*-contact-sheet.png` (before, nine spark phases from 0 to 700 ms, reduce-motion marks, after expiry), `*-sparks.mp4` and the step logs `*-steps.log`, produced headlessly by `UiFidelityGearSpark`. The batch-mode Screen is 640×480, so the portrait frame is only 221×480. The video stitches phases set through `ForgeStatSpark.Age`, so it is evidence of **where the effect appears, in what order, and that it ends**, not of real frame timing or device performance.
- Not verified: continuous playback in a real Game View, exact 440×956 / 956×440 and PC 16:9 / 16:10 / 21:9 captures, and English captures. No new text was added, so there is no English string for this unit.

**Remaining.** Other forge services (option change, slots, cores) are not extended. Device performance was not measured. For the user's "adjust it bit by bit" pass, the tuning values are `ForgeStatSpark.Life`, `Count` and the star size (`h*.3f+4`).

## U5 · Item detail, equip comparison and score (report 8)

**Request.** The detail window looks half-finished. Take Diablo IV as reference but redraw it in this game's cream, navy and gold with the rarity colours. Equip comparison must show the worn gear and the candidate on one screen, and whether the score is better or worse must read at a glance in green or red.

**Facts found in the source.**
- The old brown/gold came from `ItemDetailPopup` repainting a dark `StorageSurface` over the cream window and drawing gold title and buttons. The card body (`ItemDetailView`) was already cream and navy.
- The green/red score was already calculated (`ItemTooltip.Score`). It was a display problem: the light green/salmon hues were pushed through `UiSkin.OnLight` on cream and turned into a dark brown, the "faint red line".
- Portrait (width below 460) showed one card at a time behind tabs in `EquipmentComparisonView`, and the inventory detail did not open a comparison in portrait at all.

**Implementation.** Every call site (inventory, shop, storage, recommendation, rewards, training) shares `EquipmentComparisonView.Create`, so only that part and the detail shell changed. The rules are in [the 2026-10-22 entry of the comparison rules](../Design/Equipment_Comparison_Rules.en.md).
- Always two cards (one worn piece on the left, the candidate on the right). Two rings and main/off hand are picked with buttons for a 1:1 view. For rings the chosen position is the swap target.
- Score bands: `Gear score` and the weapon's `Weapon loadout score` are separate bands, shown by green/red/neutral plus sign, arrow, word and percentage. `ItemTooltip.ScorePair` builds the baseline in one place and `ItemTooltip.Score` uses it too. The single detail's score plaque follows the same colour rule.
- Landscape and PC use a left rail (score band, loadout band, piece-choice buttons) and two full-height cards; portrait stacks the bands on top. The footer carries the change sentence and an `Equip to {0}` button.
- `ItemDetailPopup` takes a `UiSkin` and then draws the casual window, buttons, range toggle and bubbles; call sites without a skin keep the old shell. `InventoryWindow.ShowDetail` always opens a comparison for an unequipped item (the `Equip compare` button is gone) and `ShowComparison` is a thin alias.
- The mockup was changed twice at the user's direction: (1) two cards without tabs in portrait, (2) no stacked cards in landscape, a left rail plus two cards instead. The `Equip compare` boards on the canvas show the final structure.

**Remaining and limits.**
- Terms: the user's sketch labelled the middle "comparison target" and the right "original gear". To follow the rule (worn left, candidate right) the middle shows the worn piece and the right the candidate. Tell us if the opposite was meant.
- Shop details may use the stacked structure when the host is narrow. Real captures of the shop, storage, reward, recommendation and training screens are not done yet.
- The native smokes (`RuntimeInventorySmoke*`, `RuntimeEquipmentShopSmoke.Layout`, `RuntimeFirstPlayAcceptance`) were updated for the new structure (tabs and the `Equip compare` button removed) but not run in a macOS player.

**Verification (U5).** 304 Edit Mode tests passed in batch mode: `ItemComparisonTests` 39 (5 new: one card per ring or main/off hand with the button 1:1 choice, score band sign/colour/word, rail versus stacked layout), `NativeInventoryTests` 38, `CasualUiSkinTests` 12, `RiftGearOfferWindowTests` 5, `ItemTooltipSettingsTests` 14, `RecommendedEquipmentTests` 92, `StorageCasualSkinTests` 6, `EquipmentShopTests` 30, `LocalizationTests` 34 and the one changed `SharedUiTests` case. `check_ui_contract.py --verify` passed 15 tests (colours are `UiTheme.Compare*` tokens) and `check_ui_refresh.py` passed 14 bindings.
- Captures: `GearUiEvidence20261021/compare-*.png` (Korean portrait, landscape, PC; English portrait, landscape). Each sheet covers score down, score up, a similar score, two rings, an empty slot, a two-hander (main hand + shield) and the equipped single detail. The batch-mode Screen is 640×480, so the portrait frame is only 221×480; the captures are layout and colour evidence, not exact 440×956 / 956×440, PC 16:10 / 21:9 or real touch/pointer evidence. The "equal score" capture was not exactly equal because the generated items roll different options (the neutral tone is checked by a unit test).
- Not verified: the native smokes, real captures of the shop, storage, reward, recommendation and training screens, and `RiftResultTests` / `RiftVictorySkinTests` (run alone, 15 of them fail because `RiftVictoryWindow`'s static initializer calls `Resources.Load` during MonoBehaviour construction. That run-order dependence predates this change; the only edit to that window is passing `v.Skin` to its popup).

## U6 · New form for the bulk-salvage auto-select settings (report 9)

**Request.** Replace the salvage auto-select settings window (old black/brown form, long equipment list, fixed bottom save button) with a window in the new art direction.

**Facts found in the source.** `InventoryWindow.OpenAutoSettings` opened the settings-only host of `EquipmentShopWindow.OpenAutoSettings`, which drew the old `DrawSettings`; the shop had its own new form `DrawMockSettings`.

**Implementation.**
- The settings-only host now draws `DrawMockSettings`. It builds only the window region on a bare canvas and never takes the shop's stock preparation, transaction or `Repaint` paths. Opening changes no account state (tested).
- The sentence "Shop and salvage share this character's auto-select settings" is kept. Edits apply to the draft (`draft`) and reach the setting only when a save succeeds. Cancelling or a failed save leaves the original `Hero.equipmentShop.auto`; on failure the window stays open and shows the error in the footer. Saving never salvages anything.
- Fixed footer: `Salvage targets: N` is not the shop's sale count; it applies `InventorySalvagePlan.Eligible` (equipped, locked, socketed, preset protections) and `ShopAutoSelect.MatchesFilters(draft)`, the same predicate as the salvage list. `N protected items excluded` sits under it, and the in-bag counts use the same rule.
- Weapons, armor and jewelry are folding groups (only weapons start open; a group whose detail editor is open stays open). The group checkbox switches the whole group. The shop's own dialog keeps its expanded list.
- Setting meaning and the storage key (`Hero.equipmentShop.auto`) are unchanged. The settings-only branches of the legacy dialog and their strings were removed (`DrawSettings` now serves the gamble shop only).

**Remaining and limits.** The mockup's character/level chip is not drawn because the host has no catalog. The bulk-salvage confirmation window (`ShowSalvage`) list and buttons are out of scope. Portrait keyboard exposure, scrolling in the middle/bottom of a long list and input focus were not verified in Edit Mode; scroll and focus reuse the existing `MockSettingsChanged`/`ApplySettingsOffset` path.

**Verification (U6).** `SalvageSettingsHostTests` (6 new) checks that opening alone leaves the account JSON identical, draft edits and cancel preserve the original, a save changes only this character's setting and leaves other characters, bag and gold alone, a failed save keeps the original and the window open, the count equals the salvage list (`InventorySalvagePlan.AutoSelect`) and recounts on draft edits, and group folding and the group checkbox. It is part of the 304-test batch above.
- Captures: `GearUiEvidence20261021/settings-*.png` (Korean portrait, landscape, PC; English portrait): initial state, armor group opened, axe details opened, common rarity switched off (targets 8 → 4). Not verified: portrait keyboard exposure and the save button being covered, scrolling in the middle/bottom of a long list and input focus preservation (these need a real keyboard and pointer).
- On portrait the count row stacks above the buttons, and when a rarity name is long (English) the rarity chips switch to two columns. The shop's own settings dialog uses the same rule.
