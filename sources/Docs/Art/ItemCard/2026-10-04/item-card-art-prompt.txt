# Task: ornamental art for the HELLSCRIPT item detail card (dark gothic ARPG, Diablo-IV-like)

Use the installed `imagegen` skill in its built-in `image_gen` mode. Make exactly ONE image generation call per asset below (five calls). Do not edit, recolour, crop, resize, chroma-key or remove backgrounds. Copy each generated PNG byte-for-byte from `~/.codex/generated_images/...` into `out/<id>.png`. Then write `out/manifest.json` with per asset: `id`, exact prompt used, source path, pixel size, whether it has an alpha channel, fraction of fully transparent pixels, alpha of the four corners and centre pixel, and `"model": "unknown"`. Print a one-line summary per asset.

Context: these replace plain vector rules and corners on the item tooltip card (attached `ref-card.png` shows the current card: near-black warm surface, thin gold lines). The game tints every piece at runtime with the item's rarity colour (grey / blue / yellow / orange / green), so every asset must be ONE flat pure white (#FFFFFF) shape on a native transparent background, no colour, no gradients, no glow, no shadow, no drop shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers, crisp anti-aliased edges. Look: engraved gilded gothic metalwork, delicate filigree, sharp thorn-and-leaf scrollwork, slightly ornate but readable at small size. Symmetric where stated.

Assets:
1. id `card-corner`: ONE top-left corner ornament for a card frame. Square 1:1, 1024x1024. An L-shaped bracket: two thin straight bars meeting at the top-left corner, with an ornate filigree flourish (curling thorn vines and a small diamond jewel) at the corner point and tapering to fine points along both edges. The corner point sits exactly at the top-left of the canvas with about 4% padding.
2. id `card-divider`: a horizontal ornamental divider, wide 3:1 (as wide as the tool allows). Perfectly symmetric left/right: a fine straight line tapering to points at both ends, a small ornate diamond jewel in the exact centre flanked by two mirrored curling filigree scrolls. Vertically centred with 10% padding.
3. id `power-badge`: a crest plate that sits behind a big number (the item's power score). Wide ~2:1 rectangle-ish shield/plaque shape with pointed, winged gothic ends and an engraved double border, the inner area left as a hollow (transparent) field so a number can be read through it. Symmetric.
4. id `socket-ring`: an empty gem socket seen from the front: a round ring made of an outer thin circle and an inner thicker ring with four tiny prongs at the cardinal points, the centre hollow (transparent). Square 1:1, 1024x1024, 8% padding.
5. id `greater-star`: a small emblem marking a "greater affix": a bold four-pointed star with long vertical/horizontal points and short diagonal points, with a tiny flame-like flicker rising from the top point, one solid shape. Square 1:1, 1024x1024, 10% padding.
